How Scoring Works

How a Round Works

Each daily round runs on UTC time:

  • 00:00 UTC — the round opens for forecasts.
  • 00:00 UTC next day — cutoff. Forecasts lock.
  • 00:05 UTC — settlement runs and the next round opens.

You rank 3 tokens you think will gain the most (UP) and 3 you think will fall the hardest (DOWN), each in order 1–2–3. Order matters — position #1 is worth the most points.

Scoring

Each side (UP and DOWN) is scored independently against the actual top-3 movers:

OutcomePoints
Exact position #1100

Outside top-30
  • Max per side: 220 · max per day: 440
  • Perfect day (all 6 positions exact): +60 bonus → 500

Streaks

Play consecutive days to build a streak. Each day adds +10% to your score multiplier, up to ×2.0. A 10-day streak doubles your points. Streaks count playing, not winning — consistency is rewarded.

Weekly Rounds

Separate from daily rounds, weekly rounds run Monday → Sunday. Same scoring rules, but final points are ×3. Rarer, harder, bigger rewards.

Tie-breaks & Edge Cases

  • Same % change → higher volume wins the rank.
  • Token with no trades in the window → treated as 0% change.
  • You can edit your forecast anytime before cutoff.
